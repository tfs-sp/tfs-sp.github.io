Trusted Bridge Consulting Ltd

Trusted Bridge Consulting Ltd is a UK consultancy that reduces repetitive manual work for legal and accountancy firms. We take over the coordination our clients used to do by hand, and we evidence everything we do.

Client onboarding packs

Every new client sends us an engagement letter, a client information form, and certified ID documents. We run conflict checks and AML verification against each individual, chase whatever is missing, and open the matter file once every item on our 12-point compliance checklist has passed. A partner signs the pack off before work starts.

Quarterly reporting

Each quarter we gather transaction schedules from the client's practice-management system, their bookkeeping platform and their bank feed, reconcile the three, and produce a reviewed reporting pack for the client's finance team with a variance note for every line over £500.

Renewal chasing

We track more than forty recurring renewals per client — practising certificates, insurance schedules, data processing agreements. We request updated certificates, log them, and remind the responsible partner fourteen days before anything expires.

How an engagement runs

Scoping call, signed services schedule, data-room checklist. During delivery we send a weekly status update listing outstanding documents, completed checks and blocked items. At handover the client receives a completion certificate and an indexed document bundle with an audit trail.
